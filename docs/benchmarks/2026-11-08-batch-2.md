# Performance batch 2 (2026-11-08)

Batch 1 (`21f6e42`) against `de4f67c`, measured with `xslt-benchmark/compare.sh -f 1 -wi 3 -i 5 -prof gc` at 10,000
lines for the workloads the batch changes, in both orders of the commits (load average 4 to 6). The two orders
agree on every workload; time is the mean of both runs, allocation per operation is the same in both.

The changes of the batch:

* A predicate whose value does not depend on the focus (`ExprWalker.isFocusIndependent`, failing closed: an
  explicit list of built-in functions, math and constructors, each resolved to the built-in implementation) is
  evaluated once per step or filter application when there are at least two candidates, with the focus of the
  first candidate.
* A `long` fast path for `+ - * idiv mod` on `xs:integer`, falling back to `BigInteger` on overflow.
* Argument conversion without a sequence builder and without the final type check for values that already match,
  untyped nodes atomised without a sequence, placeholders of a call site remembered.
* XSD identity constraints: selectors and fields of the XSD subset (Part 1 section 3.11.6) parsed once and walked on
  the tree (`XsdIdentityPath`), other forms through the compiled XPath; the depth walk and the copies of assertion
  trees without a record per node.
